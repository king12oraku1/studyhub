Quick Help

What do you need help with?

Lecture Notes

Learn how to find, view and download lecture notes for your courses. Notes are organised by college, programme, level and semester.

Past Questions

Access previous examination papers organised by course and year. Use the filters to narrow down to your specific course.

Practice Quizzes

Configure timed practice quizzes by selecting your college, programme, level and course. Review your answers and explanations after each quiz.

Student Profile

Set up your profile with your name, academic level and programme. StudyHub uses this to personalise your course recommendations.

Course Discovery

Browse all available courses using the Courses page. Filter by college, programme, level and semester, or search by course code or title.

Technical Issues

If a PDF won't load, try refreshing the page or opening it in a different browser. If a problem persists, contact us by email.

How to access lecture notes

Navigate to Study Materials → Lecture Notes in the top navigation, or tap Notes in the mobile bottom bar.

  • Select your College from the dropdown to filter by institution.
  • Select your Programme. This list updates based on the college you chose.
  • Choose your Level and Semester.
  • Pick a specific Course from the final dropdown.
  • Click View to open the PDF in a new tab, or Download to save it.

You can also search by course code or title using the search bar at the top of the filter panel.

How to access past questions

Navigate to Study Materials → Past Questions in the top navigation, or tap Past Questions in the mobile bottom bar.

  • Use the filter bar to select College, Programme, Level, Semester, Course and Year.
  • Click View Paper to open the examination paper in a new tab.
  • Click Download to save the paper to your device.

Not all courses have past questions uploaded yet. Papers are added as they become available.

How to take a practice quiz

Navigate to Practice in the navigation bar.

  • Step 1 — Select Your Course: Choose your College, Programme, Level, Semester and Course.
  • Step 2 — Configure: Select how many questions you want and your preferred duration.
  • Step 3 — Review & Start: Check the summary and click Start Practice Quiz.
  • During the quiz, use the question navigator dots to jump between questions.
  • When you are ready to finish, click Submit Quiz and confirm.
  • After submission you will see your score, correct answers and explanations for every question.

How to set up and edit your profile

Your profile appears automatically the first time you visit StudyHub on a new device. You can change it at any time.

  • Click your name or the profile button in the top-right corner of any page.
  • Select Edit Profile from the dropdown.
  • Update your name, academic level or department/programme.
  • Click Save Changes. The homepage will update immediately to show your new personalised courses.

Note: Your profile is stored only on your current device using browser local storage. Clearing your browser data or switching devices will require you to set up your profile again.

Supported browsers

StudyHub works best in modern browsers. We recommend:

  • Google Chrome (latest version)
  • Mozilla Firefox (latest version)
  • Apple Safari (iOS 15 and above)
  • Microsoft Edge (latest version)

JavaScript must be enabled for all features to work. PDF viewing requires a browser with built-in PDF support or a PDF viewer plugin.

PDF files are not loading

If a PDF fails to open or download, try the following steps:

  • Refresh the page and try again.
  • Try a different browser.
  • Check that you have a working internet connection.
  • If the issue persists, the file may not yet be uploaded. Contact us and we will investigate.
