Privacy Policy

How StudyHub handles your information.

Last updated: November 2026

The short version: StudyHub is a static website. We do not collect, store or transmit any personal data to any server. The only information saved about you is stored locally in your own browser on your own device.

1. Who we are

StudyHub is a free, static educational platform designed for university students. The platform is accessible at studiyhub.netlify.app and hosted on Netlify's static hosting infrastructure. StudyHub is not a registered company and operates as a student resource project.

Contact: studiyhub@gmail.com

2. Information we do not collect

StudyHub does not collect, process or store any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses or any personal contact information on our servers
  • Academic records or institutional identifiers
  • Payment information of any kind
  • IP addresses beyond what is automatically processed by Netlify's hosting infrastructure
  • Browser fingerprints or device identifiers
  • Usage behaviour, click patterns or analytics events
  • Cookies set by StudyHub itself

No user database exists. No account creation is required. We have no mechanism to identify individual users.

3. Information stored on your device

StudyHub stores two categories of data in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device.

Student profile

When you complete the onboarding setup, the following is saved to localStorage under the key studiyhub_student_profile:

  • Your first name (as you typed it)
  • Your selected academic level (100–500)
  • Your selected department or programme
  • Timestamps for when the profile was created and last updated

This data is used exclusively to personalise your homepage — displaying relevant courses, greeting you by name and pre-filling filters on the notes and past questions pages. It is never transmitted to any external system.

Filter preferences

Your most recent course filter selections (college, programme, level, semester) are stored in localStorage under the key studyhubFilters so they are remembered when you return to the same page. This data is also never transmitted externally.

Theme preference

Your chosen visual theme (light or dark mode) is stored in localStorage under the key theme.

Removing your stored data

You can remove all locally stored data at any time by:

  • Clicking your profile name in the navigation and selecting Reset Profile, which removes your profile data.
  • Clearing your browser's site data or local storage for studiyhub.netlify.app through your browser settings.

4. How your profile data is used

Your locally stored profile is used only to:

  • Display a personalised greeting on the homepage
  • Show course cards relevant to your programme and level
  • Pre-select matching filters on the Notes and Past Questions pages
  • Show your name and profile details in the navigation dropdown

No profiling, targeting or sharing of this information occurs.

5. Cookies and tracking

StudyHub does not set any cookies. We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any other third-party analytics, tracking or advertising tools.

Netlify, our hosting provider, may log standard web server request data (such as IP address and request timestamps) for security and operational purposes in accordance with their own privacy policy at netlify.com/privacy. This data is not shared with StudyHub.

6. Third-party services

StudyHub loads the following resources from external CDNs when you visit the site:

  • Google Fonts — to load the Inter typeface. Google's servers may log the request. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • KaTeX (jsDelivr CDN) — for rendering mathematical notation in quiz questions.
  • PDF.js (Cloudflare CDN) — for in-browser PDF rendering.

These services are used only for rendering content. We do not share any user data with them and do not control their data practices.

7. External links

StudyHub may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies when you visit them.

8. Children's privacy

StudyHub is designed for university students aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through this platform, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of StudyHub after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact us

If you have questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us:

  • Email: studiyhub@gmail.com
